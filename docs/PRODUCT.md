# Product Overview

## The Problem

Professionals have access to more AI content than ever, but the learning experience is fragmented. People can learn prompting, try new tools, watch tutorials, and earn certificates without knowing how those pieces fit together or what capability they should develop next.

Your Next Skill is designed around progression rather than content volume.

## The Experience

The platform helps users establish a starting point, explore an appropriate learning path, complete focused lessons, and move toward practical application.

The experience is designed for professionals who may not have a technical background but increasingly need AI fluency in their work.

## Audience

Initial audiences include:

- Business professionals building practical AI fluency
- HR and talent professionals
- Learning and enablement leaders
- Small and midsize business teams
- Professionals responsible for introducing AI into existing workflows

## Skill Model

Users can self-assess across four broad stages:

1. Beginner
2. Intermediate
3. Advanced
4. Highly Skilled

The model is intentionally understandable rather than overly technical. Its purpose is to help users orient themselves and choose a useful next step.

## Design Approach

Your Next Skill combines AI literacy, hands-on learning, workflow thinking, and organizational enablement. The product is being developed iteratively through beta feedback and real-world use.

## Public Showcase vs. Production Application

This GitHub repository documents the product and its design approach. The production application, protected learning content, user information, credentials, API keys, and private application configuration are not published here.
